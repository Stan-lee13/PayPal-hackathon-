## 1. Key findings (top 10)

1. **2024 was the year US regulators turned on the bank-fintech partnership model (BaaS).** Synapse filed for bankruptcy in April 2024. End users were owed about $265m against about $180m that partner banks could trace, so roughly $65–96m was missing. Over the same months Evolve, Thread, Sutton, Piermont, Lineage and Blue Ridge received consent orders. VERIFIED ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/synapse-trustee-85-million-shortfall-customer-funds/718796/); [Fed on Evolve][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20240614a.htm); [Banking Dive on Thread][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/fdic-thread-bank-baas-oversight-fintech-partner-aml/720313/)).
2. **TD Bank paid about $3.09bn and pleaded guilty to Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) violations and to money-laundering conspiracy (10 Oct 2024).** This was the largest BSA penalty ever. TD had left trillions of dollars of transactions unmonitored each year, Zelle and Venmo flows included, and it accepted a growth restriction on its US business. VERIFIED ([FinCEN][F](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-assesses-record-13-billion-penalty-against-td-bank); [Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20241010a.htm); [OCC][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-116.html)).
3. **The CFPB's most aggressive rulemaking year was largely undone within 12 months.** It finalised a $8 credit-card late-fee cap (blocked by a court in May 2024), a $5 overdraft rule (nullified by Congress in May 2025), the 1033 open-banking rule (sued the same day, enjoined in October 2025), supervision of large payment apps, and the Zelle lawsuit (dismissed in March 2025). Most of this happened even though the Supreme Court upheld the CFPB's funding 7–2 in May 2024. VERIFIED ([CFPB late fee][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-bans-excessive-credit-card-late-fees-lowers-typical-fee-from-32-to-8/); [SCOTUS][F](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-448_o7jp.pdf); [CFPB Zelle page][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-sues-jpmorgan-chase-bank-of-america-and-wells-fargo-for-allowing-fraud-to-fester-on-zelle/)).
4. **Payments fraud hit record levels.** The FBI's IC3 recorded $16.6bn in losses for 2024 ($12.5bn in 2023), with crypto investment fraud at $9.3bn. UNODC put 2023 scam losses in East and Southeast Asia at $18–37bn. Chainalysis found pig-butchering revenue up about 40% in 2024. VERIFIED ([IC3 2024][F](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2024_IC3Report.pdf); [UNODC][F](https://www.unodc.org/roseap/uploads/documents/Publications/2024/TOC_Convergence_Report_2024.pdf); [Chainalysis][F](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2024-pig-butchering-scam-revenue-grows-yoy/)).
5. **The UK made reimbursement of authorised push payment (APP) fraud mandatory on 7 Oct 2024, with a cap of £85,000 per claim.** The cap was lowered from the originally proposed £415k. VERIFIED ([PSR PS24/7][F](https://www.psr.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps247-faster-payments-app-scams-reimbursement-requirement-confirming-the-maximum-level-of-reimbursement/)).
6. **Nigeria's regulators hit fintech growth from several directions in 2024.**
   - Accounts without BVN/NIN were frozen from 1 Mar 2024.
   - The naira fell more than 50% in a week (Feb 2024).
   - A 0.5% cybersecurity levy on e-transfers was ordered on 6 May and withdrawn 11 days later.
   - OPay, PalmPay, Kuda and Moniepoint (and Paga) were barred from onboarding new customers for about six weeks.
   - Banks were ordered to recapitalise (₦500bn for international licences).
   - Agent cash-out caps were imposed in December.

   VERIFIED, mostly from primary CBN documents ([CBN Tier-1 circular][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2023/PSMD/Circular%20on%20Tier%201%20Wallets%20%26%20Accounts,%20Guidance%20Note%20%26%20Profiling%20of%20Customers%27%20Accounts%20%26%20Wallets.pdf); [CBN recap][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/CCD/Recapitalization_MARCH_2024.pdf); [TechCabal][F](https://techcabal.com/2024/06/03/cbn-lifts-onboarding-freeze/)).
7. **Concentration risk in tech and third-party providers became visible across the industry.**
   - CrowdStrike's faulty update (19 Jul 2024) hit about 8.5m Windows devices and disrupted banks on several continents.
   - Data was stolen from Finastra, which serves 45 of the top 50 banks.
   - Evolve leaked data on 7.64m people.
   - Patelco's ransomware attack locked out about half a million members.

   VERIFIED ([Microsoft][F](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2024/07/20/helping-our-customers-through-the-crowdstrike-outage/); [Krebs][F](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2024/11/fintech-giant-finastra-investigating-data-breach/); [SecurityWeek][F](https://www.securityweek.com/evolve-bank-data-breach-impacts-7-6-million-people/)).
8. **Antitrust pressure on the card networks rose.**
   - Capital One agreed to buy Discover for $35.3bn (19 Feb 2024), which would give it its own card network.
   - Judge Brodie refused to give preliminary approval to the Visa/Mastercard injunctive-relief settlement on **25 Jun 2024**. The brief said November; that date is wrong.
   - The DOJ sued Visa on 24 Sep 2024 for monopolising debit.

   VERIFIED ([Visa 10-K][F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316124000058/v-20240930.htm); [CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/02/19/capital-one-acquiring-discover-financial-services-report-says.html)).
9. **US banks came through 2024 with only two failures, but stress built underneath.**
   - Republic First failed (Apr 2024, cost to the deposit insurance fund $667m), and so did First National Bank of Lindsay (Oct 2024).
   - NYCB needed a $1bn+ rescue led by Steven Mnuchin's firm.
   - Unrealised securities losses reached $482bn at end-2024.
   - Office commercial real estate (CRE) was still stressed.
   - The Fed cut rates by 100bp from September.

   VERIFIED ([FDIC][F](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/fulton-bank-na-lancaster-pennsylvania-assumes-substantially-all-deposits); [FDIC failed-bank list][F](https://www.fdic.gov/bank-failures/failed-bank-list); [OCC risk report][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-135.html)).
10. **Fintech split between scaled, profitable winners and a funding drought.**
    - Fintech venture funding fell to a seven-year low of $33.7bn.
    - Monzo (£15.4m) and Nubank ($2.0bn) reported profits.
    - Adyen's processed volume grew 33%.
    - PayPal grew TPV 10% to $1.68tn but active accounts only 2%.
    - Klarna claimed its AI assistant did the work of 700 agents.

    VERIFIED/REPORTED ([CB Insights][F](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/fintech-trends-2024/); [PayPal 10-K][F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391725000019/pypl-20241231.htm); [Klarna][F](https://www.klarna.com/international/press/klarna-ai-assistant-handles-two-thirds-of-customer-service-chats-in-its-first-month/)).

## 2. Detailed findings

### 2.1 US bank stability: failures, CRE, unrealised losses, rates

**Republic First Bank (failed 26 Apr 2024).**
- Pennsylvania regulators closed the Philadelphia bank. It had about $6bn of assets and $4bn of deposits.
- Fulton Bank took over substantially all deposits and assets, and the 32 branches reopened under Fulton.
- The FDIC estimated the cost to the Deposit Insurance Fund at **$667m**. It was the first US failure since November 2023. VERIFIED ([FDIC][F](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/fulton-bank-na-lancaster-pennsylvania-assumes-substantially-all-deposits)).
- An earlier plan to recapitalise the bank with $35m had collapsed. REPORTED ([CBS][S](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/republic-bank-failure-fulton-bank-pa-nj-ny-fdic)).
- The only other 2024 failure was First National Bank of Lindsay, Oklahoma (18 Oct 2024). VERIFIED ([FDIC list][F](https://www.fdic.gov/bank-failures/failed-bank-list)).
- Cause, INTERPRETATION: a mid-size bank with underwater securities and CRE exposure that could not raise capital once rates stayed high. This is the same mechanism as the 2023 failures, at smaller scale.

**NYCB/Flagstar (Jan–Mar 2024).**
- On 31 Jan 2024 NYCB reported a Q4 2023 loss of about $252m, a **$552m provision**, and a dividend cut from $0.17 to $0.05. The shares fell about 38% that day. The reserve build was tied to CRE and to NYCB moving into the >$100bn regulatory category after it bought Signature Bank's assets. REPORTED ([Zacks][S](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2219794/new-york-community-nycb-swings-to-loss-in-q4-stock-tanks-377)).
- On 6 Mar 2024 investors led by Mnuchin's Liberty Strategic Capital put in about $1.05bn: Liberty $450m, Hudson Bay $250m, Reverence $200m, at $2 per share. Joseph Otting became CEO. VERIFIED via SEC 8-K (snippet) and trade press ([SEC 8-K][S](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910073/000119312524061476/d787107dex993.htm); [NMN][S](https://www.nationalmortgagenews.com/news/new-york-community-lands-1b-capital-infusion-led-by-mnuchin-otting)).

**Stress test, June 2024.**
- The Fed tested 31 banks against a severe scenario. Projected losses were about **$685bn**, including about $80bn from CRE and $175bn from credit cards.
- The aggregate CET1 capital ratio fell 2.8pp, from 12.7% to 9.9%. Every bank stayed above its minimum. VERIFIED ([Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20240626a.htm)).

**Unrealised losses and problem banks.**
- Unrealised securities losses were **$482.4bn** at Q4 2024, up $118.4bn in the quarter as long-term rates rose again.
- Problem banks fell from 68 to 66. REPORTED ([FDIC statement][S](https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2025/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-fourth-quarter-2024)). I confirmed the problem-bank count on the FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile page ([QBP][F](https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile/quarterly-banking-profile-q4-2024)).
- The OCC's Fall 2024 risk report said office CRE "remains stressed" and luxury multifamily risk "elevated". Card and auto delinquencies were rising and operational risk was "elevated". VERIFIED ([OCC][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-135.html)).

**Rate cuts.**
- 18 Sep: −50bp to 4.75–5.00%. The vote was 11–1; Governor Bowman preferred a 25bp cut.
- 7 Nov: −25bp to 4.50–4.75%.
- 18 Dec: −25bp to 4.25–4.50%.
- VERIFIED ([Sep][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20240918a.htm); [Nov][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20241107a.htm); [Dec][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20241218a.htm)).
- Deposits: the FDIC Q4 2024 profile says deposits grew, but I could not extract the exact figures (gap).

**Basel III endgame stalls.**
- On 10 Sep 2024 Fed Vice Chair for Supervision Michael Barr previewed a re-proposal. The capital increase for the largest banks (G-SIBs) would be about **9%** instead of the original plan.
- Banks with $100–250bn of assets would be exempt, except that they would have to count unrealised securities gains and losses (AOCI) in capital, adding about 3–4% over time. VERIFIED ([Barr speech][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/barr20240910a.htm)).
- No final rule came in 2024. INTERPRETATION: the post-SVB lesson about recognising unrealised losses survived politically, but the overall capital increase was cut by more than half.

### 2.2 Bank-fintech partnerships (BaaS): Synapse, Evolve and the consent-order wave

**What happened.**
- Synapse was an a16z-backed middleware firm that sat between fintech apps and partner banks (Evolve, AMG National Trust, American Bank, Lineage). It filed for Chapter 11 in April 2024.
- A dispute with Evolve, and Synapse cutting off access to its ledger in May, froze accounts across roughly 100 fintechs. Jason Mikula estimated as many as 10m end customers ([TechCrunch][F](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/25/with-a16z-backed-synapses-collapse-baas-fintech-is-a-mess-and-10-million-consumers-could-be-hurt/)).
- The trustee is former FDIC Chair Jelena McWilliams. In June she reported that end users were owed about **$265m** while banks held about **$180m**. More than 100,000 people had been locked out, including about 85,000 Yotta customers ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/synapse-trustee-85-million-shortfall-customer-funds/718796/)).

**Consequences.**
- By 12 Nov the banks had released $193m.
- Yotta's 13,725 customers were offered **$11.8m against $64.9m** of deposits. Individual cases included $94,468.92 deposited and $128.68 offered.
- The FDIC said its insurance fund does not cover the failure of a nonbank. The judge said: "There's nothing optimistic about what I'm telling you." VERIFIED ([CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/22/synapse-bankruptcy-thousands-of-americans-see-their-savings-vanish.html)).

**Why it happened.**
- Customer funds sat in pooled "for benefit of" (FBO) accounts. The ledger showing who owned what was kept by an uninsured middleman, and no one reconciled it against the banks' own records.
- Evolve could identify the funds it held but not where the missing money went, because of "very large bulk transfers" made without owner identification ([CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/22/synapse-bankruptcy-thousands-of-americans-see-their-savings-vanish.html)).

**Regulatory response.**
- **Evolve:** Fed and Arkansas cease-and-desist order on 14 Jun 2024. It cited the lack of an effective risk-management framework for fintech partnerships, plus AML and consumer-compliance gaps ([Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20240614a.htm)).
- **Thread Bank:** FDIC consent order dated 21 May, made public 28 June. It required risk tolerances per fintech partner, approved by the board, and an exit plan covering 3rd-, 4th- and 5th-party providers ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/fdic-thread-bank-baas-oversight-fintech-partner-aml/720313/)).
- **Sutton** (1 Feb) and **Piermont** (26 Feb) received FDIC orders requiring AML lookbacks ([Banking Dive][S](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/piermont-sutton-bank-fdic-consent-orders-baas-fintech/711815/)).
- **FDIC custodial-account rule:** proposed 17 Sep 2024 (RIN 3064-AG07). Banks would have to keep records identifying the beneficial owner of every custodial account with transactional features, reconcile them, and have continuity plans ([Federal Register][S](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-10-02/html/2024-22565.htm)).

**Evolve breach.**
- LockBit was in Evolve's network from at least February and deployed ransomware on 29 May 2024.
- Evolve told the Maine Attorney General that **7,640,112** people were affected, including customers of its open-banking partners. VERIFIED ([SecurityWeek][F](https://www.securityweek.com/evolve-bank-data-breach-impacts-7-6-million-people/); [The Record][F](https://therecord.media/evolve-bank-data-breach-lockbit)).
- The same small bank failed on compliance, on cyber security, and on reconciliation at once.

Evidence label: VERIFIED. The shortfall range is DISPUTED/unsettled (see section 3).

### 2.3 AML and enforcement

**TD Bank (10 Oct 2024).**
- Penalties across agencies totalled about **$3.09bn** ([Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20241010a.htm)): FinCEN's record **$1.3bn**, the OCC's $450m, the Fed's $123.5m, and the DOJ criminal resolution.
- FinCEN found that TD:
  - let "trillions of dollars" of transactions a year go unmonitored;
  - failed to file suspicious activity reports (SARs) on about **$1.5bn**;
  - did not report P2P flows (Zelle/Venmo) that showed signs of human trafficking;
  - processed more than $400m for one launderer (Da Ying Sze) ([FinCEN][F](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-assesses-record-13-billion-penalty-against-td-bank)).
- The OCC cited a "persistent prioritization of growth over controls" and imposed a growth restriction ([OCC][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-116.html)).
- Secondary reports put the US asset cap at $434bn ([Al Jazeera][S](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2024/10/10/td-bank-pleads-guilty-to-us-charges-faces-business-restrictions)). I could not open DOJ's own release, which is behind a bot wall.

**Other 2024 actions.**
- **Citi (10 Jul):** about $135.6m combined (Fed $60.6m, OCC $75m) for "insufficient progress" on data-quality fixes required by its 2020 orders ([Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20240710a.htm)).
- **Wells Fargo (12 Sep):** OCC Formal Agreement on SAR/CTR reporting, customer due diligence and beneficial ownership ([OCC][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-99.html)). It needs OCC approval before launching new products or entering new markets with medium or high BSA/AML risk ([Compliance Week][S](https://complianceweek.com/regulatory-enforcement/wells-fargo-agrees-with-occ-to-shore-up-bsa/aml-sanctions-program-deficiencies/35371.article)).
- **Bank of America (23 Dec):** OCC cease-and-desist over late SARs and an uncorrected customer-due-diligence deficiency ([OCC][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-140.html)).
- **USAA FSB (18 Dec):** comprehensive order replacing those of 2019 and 2022, covering fraud and third-party risk management ([OCC][F](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-137.html)).

**Payment apps (resolved in January 2025).**
- The CFPB ordered Block/Cash App to pay up to $120m in redress plus a $55m penalty (16 Jan 2025). It found weak security and that Block closed fraud disputes without investigating them; Cash App has more than 56m accounts ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-orders-operator-of-cash-app-to-pay-175-million-and-fix-its-failures-on-fraud/)).
- 48 state regulators fined Block $80m over BSA/AML failures (15 Jan 2025) ([CSBS][F](https://www.csbs.org/newsroom/state-regulators-issue-80-million-penalty-block-inc-cash-app-bsaaml-violations)).

**UK challenger banks.**
- The FCA fined **Starling £28.96m** (2 Oct 2024). Starling had opened more than 54,000 accounts for 49,000 high-risk customers despite a restriction, and from 2017 its sanctions screening checked against only a fraction of the list ([FCA][F](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-fines-starling-bank-failings-financial-crime-systems-and-controls)).
- Correction to the brief: the **Monzo £21.1m fine was published on 8 Jul 2025, not in 2024**. It covers conduct from 2018–2022 ([FCA][F](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-fines-monzo-21m-failings-financial-crime-controls)).

INTERPRETATION: in 2024 enforcement moved from "fix your program" to "growth caps and criminal liability" (TD, Wells, USAA). The common failure was controls not keeping pace with growth, at TD, Starling, Monzo, Evolve and Cash App alike.

### 2.4 CFPB 2024: maximal rulemaking, minimal survival

**Funding upheld.** *CFPB v. CFSA* (16 May 2024): the Court held that funding the CFPB from Federal Reserve earnings satisfies the Appropriations Clause. Justice Thomas wrote for the 7–2 majority ([SCOTUS][F](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-448_o7jp.pdf)).

**Late fees (5 Mar).**
- Issuers with more than 1m accounts would be limited to an $8 safe-harbour fee, down from about $32.
- The CFPB said late fees cost consumers more than $14bn a year and estimated savings above $10bn ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-bans-excessive-credit-card-late-fees-lowers-typical-fee-from-32-to-8/)).
- Judge Pittman (N.D. Tex.) enjoined the rule just before its 14 May effective date and refused to lift the injunction in December ([Ballard Spahr][S](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2024/12/18/federal-judge-refuses-to-lift-injunction-blocking-cfpb-credit-card-late-fee-rule/)).

**BNPL interpretive rule (22 May).** BNPL lenders count as card issuers under Regulation Z, so customers get dispute and refund rights ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-takes-action-to-ensure-consumers-can-dispute-charges-and-obtain-refunds-on-buy-now-pay-later-loans/)). Klarna publicly objected the same day ([Klarna newsroom][F](https://www.klarna.com/international/press/)).

**Open banking, Section 1033 (22 Oct).**
- Providers must give consumers free access to, and transfer of, their data from accounts, cards, wallets and payment apps.
- Compliance deadlines ran from 1 Apr 2026 (largest firms) to 2030 ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-finalizes-personal-financial-data-rights-rule-to-boost-competition-protect-privacy-and-give-families-more-choice-in-financial-services/)).
- The Bank Policy Institute, the Kentucky Bankers Association and Forcht Bank sued the same day ([Banking Dive][S](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/bank-trade-groups-challenge-cfpb-open-banking-rule-lawsuit/730786/)).
- In May 2025 the CFPB itself called the rule unlawful. A court enjoined it on 29 Oct 2025 ([ABA][S](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2025/11/kentucky-federal-court-enjoins-cfpb-from-enforcing-current-1033-final-rule/)).

**Larger-participant rule for payment apps (21 Nov).**
- Nonbank wallet and transfer apps handling more than **50m transactions a year** became subject to CFPB supervision. The covered apps process more than 13bn transactions a year.
- The stated focus was privacy, fraud and "debanking" ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-finalizes-rule-on-federal-oversight-of-popular-digital-payment-apps-to-protect-personal-data-reduce-fraud-and-stop-illegal-debanking/)).
- Whether this rule was later repealed is outside what I verified in this slice.

**Overdraft (12 Dec).**
- Banks and credit unions with more than $10bn of assets could charge $5, charge a fee limited to cost, or treat overdraft as credit under TILA. Estimated savings were up to $5bn a year ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-closes-overdraft-loophole-to-save-americans-billions-in-fees/)).
- Banks sued the same day. Congress nullified the rule under the Congressional Review Act on 9 May 2025 ([Ballotpedia][S](https://ballotpedia.org/Overdraft_Lending:_Very_Large_Financial_Institutions_(2024))).

**Zelle lawsuit (20 Dec).**
- The CFPB sued Early Warning Services, JPMorgan, Bank of America and Wells Fargo, alleging more than **$870m** of customer losses over seven years ([Payments Dive][S](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cfpb-lawsuit-early-warning-services-zelle-payments-fraud-banks/736163/)).
- EWS called the suit "meritless" and the figure unconfirmed. Bank of America cited a 99.95% clean-transaction rate ([PII][S](https://paymentsindustryintelligence.com/cfpb-takes-on-big-us-banks-over-zelle-fraud-allegations/)).
- The CFPB dismissed the case with prejudice on 4 Mar 2025 ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-sues-jpmorgan-chase-bank-of-america-and-wells-fargo-for-allowing-fraud-to-fester-on-zelle/)).

INTERPRETATION: 2024 shows the main risk is regulatory reversal: a whole rulebook was written and then mostly unwound. The problems the rules targeted (fees, P2P scam losses, data portability, nonbank supervision) were still unresolved going into 2025–26.

### 2.5 Card networks, consolidation and antitrust

**Capital One–Discover.**
- All-stock deal announced 19 Feb 2024: 1.0192 Capital One shares per Discover share, about a 26% premium, valued at $35.3bn ([CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/02/19/capital-one-acquiring-discover-financial-services-report-says.html)).
- It closed on 18 May 2025, bringing the Discover, Pulse and Diners networks in-house ([Wikipedia][F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_One)).
- INTERPRETATION: this is the first vertical issuer-plus-network challenger to Visa and Mastercard in decades.

**Interchange settlement.**
- On 25 Mar 2024 Visa and Mastercard agreed with the merchant injunctive-relief class to reduce and cap US credit interchange and to loosen surcharging rules.
- **On 25 Jun 2024 the court denied preliminary approval** ([Visa FY2024 10-K][F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316124000058/v-20240930.htm)).

**DOJ v. Visa.**
- Filed 24 Sep 2024 in SDNY. It alleges monopolisation of general-purpose debit and card-not-present debit through agreements with merchants and acquirers ([Visa 10-K][F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316124000058/v-20240930.htm)).
- Widely reported figures (Visa holding about 60% of US debit, about $7bn a year in debit fees) could not be checked after the search budget ran out. NOT VERIFIED in this slice.

**Outside the US.** The UK PSR proposed scheme-fee transparency remedies (May 2024), and the European Commission opened a probe into Visa/Mastercard scheme fees (Nov 2024) ([Wikipedia][F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_Inc.)).

**Apple NFC.** On 11 Jul 2024 the European Commission made Apple's commitments binding. Third-party wallets in the EEA get free NFC access on iOS through Host Card Emulation (HCE). Non-compliance can be fined up to 10% of turnover ([EC][F](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_24_3706)).

### 2.6 UK

**APP fraud reimbursement.**
- Mandatory from **7 Oct 2024** for Faster Payments, with a cap of **£85,000 per claim**. The PSR says this fully covers 99.8% of cases by volume and 90% by value.
- The Bank of England set the same cap for CHAPS ([PSR PS24/7][F](https://www.psr.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps247-faster-payments-app-scams-reimbursement-requirement-confirming-the-maximum-level-of-reimbursement/)).
- The cap was cut from £415k after industry argued about prudential risk, especially for smaller payment firms. The £415k figure is from my background knowledge; the PSR page confirms only that the cap was "reduced".

**Motor finance.**
- On 25 Oct 2024 the Court of Appeal (*Johnson v FirstRand; Hopcraft/Wrench v Close Brothers*, [2024] EWCA Civ 1282) ruled for the customers on bribery and dishonest assistance. Johnson also won under the Consumer Credit Act's unfair-relationship test.
- The Supreme Court accepted the lenders' appeal on 22 Nov 2024, with the FCA intervening ([UKSC][F](https://www.supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2024-0157)).
- The industry exposure figures (tens of billions of pounds) are not verified here.

**Revolut.** Received a UK banking licence with restrictions ("mobilisation") in July 2024 and passed 50m customers by November ([fintech.global][F](https://fintech.global/2024/11/19/revolut-surpasses-50m-global-customers-aims-for-100m-milestone/)). The full licence came only on 11 Mar 2026, about 20 months later ([Wikipedia][F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolut)).

**Monzo.** FY to Mar 2024: first full-year profit of £15.4m, revenue £880m, deposits £11.2bn (+88%), more than 9.3m customers ([Monzo][F](https://monzo.com/annual-report/2024)).

**Gaps.** Consumer Duty, NatWest/Coutts debanking, and UK Finance fraud data were not covered (UK Finance blocked access; Guardian blocked).

### 2.11 Africa

**Nigeria.**
- **Currency and macro:** the naira fell from about ₦898 to ₦1,400 per dollar between 1 and 5 Feb 2024 and drifted to about ₦1,600 by July ([Wikipedia][F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_naira)).
- **KYC (CBN circular of 1 Dec 2023):**
  - all Tier-1 accounts and wallets must carry a BVN or NIN;
  - funded accounts without one were placed on "Post No Debit or Credit" from **1 Mar 2024**;
  - BVN/NIN had to be revalidated by 31 Jan 2024 ([CBN][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2023/PSMD/Circular%20on%20Tier%201%20Wallets%20%26%20Accounts,%20Guidance%20Note%20%26%20Profiling%20of%20Customers%27%20Accounts%20%26%20Wallets.pdf); I read the scanned PDF visually).
- **Bank recapitalisation (28 Mar 2024):**
  - minimum capital of ₦500bn (international), ₦200bn (national) and ₦50bn (regional) for commercial banks;
  - paid-in capital only;
  - 1 Apr 2024 to 31 Mar 2026;
  - framed as supporting a $1tn economy by 2030 ([CBN][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/CCD/Recapitalization_MARCH_2024.pdf)).
- **Cybersecurity levy:** the CBN ordered a **0.5% levy on electronic transactions** on 6 May 2024 and **withdrew it on 17 May** after public backlash ([CBN 6 May][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/CCD/CIRCULAR%20REF%20PSMDIRPUBLAB017004%2006052024.pdf); [CBN 17 May][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/CCD/WITHDRAWAL%20CIRCULAR%20ON%20CYBERSECURITY%20LEVY%2017052024.pdf)).
- **Fintech onboarding freeze:**
  - On 29 Apr 2024 the CBN ordered OPay, PalmPay, Kuda and Moniepoint (and Paga) to stop onboarding. This followed claims about illicit FX and crypto trading and an EFCC freeze of 1,146 accounts. The fintechs said most of those accounts were at commercial banks ([Nairametrics][F](https://nairametrics.com/2024/04/29/forex-deals-cbn-stops-4-fintechs-from-onboarding-new-customers/)).
  - The freeze was lifted on 3 Jun after about six weeks. Conditions: block P2P crypto transfers, verify physical addresses for all account tiers, and update facial verification ([TechCabal][F](https://techcabal.com/2024/06/03/cbn-lifts-onboarding-freeze/)).
- **Crypto:** in January 2024 the CBN allowed banks to open accounts for virtual-asset service providers, reversing the 2021 restriction. Banks still may not trade crypto on their own account ([CBN][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/FPRD/GUIDELINES%20ON%20OPERATIONS%20OF%20BANK%20ACCOUNTS%20FOR%20VIRTUAL%20Asset%20Providers.pdf)). Within months the NSA was treating crypto as a security threat (above). The policy contradicted itself within a year.
- **Cash and agents:**
  - Late 2024 cash scarcity led the CBN to order banks to dispense cash over the counter and through ATMs, and to ask the public to report branches that did not (29 Nov 2024) ([CBN][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/CCD/CIRCULAR%20ON%20CASH%20AVAILABILITY.pdf)).
  - Agent cash-out limits (17 Dec 2024): ₦100,000 per customer per day, ₦500,000 per customer per week, and ₦1.2m per agent per day. All terminals must connect through a PTSA, and agents must use merchant category code 6010 ([CBN][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/CCD/Circular%20on%20Cash-out%20limits%20for%20Agent%20Banking%20Transactions.pdf)).
- **Growth despite all this:**
  - PalmPay: more than 35m users, 1.2m businesses, up to 15m transactions a day ([TechCabal][F](https://techcabal.com/2024/11/06/how-palmpay-became-a-household-name/)).
  - Moniepoint became a unicorn with a $110m Series C (Oct 2024) ([Wikipedia][F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moniepoint)).
  - Flutterwave was reported (TechCabal, Feb 2024) to have been defrauded of up to $24m spread across more than 6,000 bank accounts ([Wikipedia][F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flutterwave)).

**Kenya.**

## 4. Evolution versus 2023 and 2025–26

- **US bank failures:** 2023 had three huge failures (SVB, Signature, First Republic). 2024 had two small ones plus the NYCB scare. The causes (rate-driven unrealised losses and CRE) carried over, and unrealised losses were still $482bn at end-2024.
- **Enforcement:** 2023's focus on liquidity and supervision became, in 2024, a focus on AML failures and fintech partnerships, with record penalties. In 2025 US federal enforcement and the CFPB's agenda were sharply rolled back: the Zelle case dropped (Mar 2025), the overdraft rule nullified (May 2025), and the 1033 rule disowned (May 2025). Block's settlements in January 2025 were the last big act of the 2024 posture.
- **Fraud:** IC3 losses went from $12.5bn (2023) to $16.6bn (2024). The UK moved from voluntary reimbursement (the CRM code) to mandatory reimbursement.
- **Neobank licensing:** Revolut's 2024 licence came with restrictions and only became a full licence in March 2026. N26's 2024 release from BaFin's cap was followed by new BaFin restrictions in December 2025. INTERPRETATION: compliance debt from 2018–2022 hyper-growth was still being paid off in 2026.
- **Nigeria:** after the 2023 naira-redesign cash crunch, 2024 brought devaluation, the KYC freeze, the onboarding freeze and recapitalisation. The recapitalisation deadline was 31 Mar 2026.

## 5. Implications for PayPal and the wider industry

- **PayPal (INTERPRETATION grounded in the 10-K).**
  - In 2024 PayPal grew volume through low-margin unbranded processing while active accounts grew only 2%.
  - The CFPB's larger-participant rule (over 50m transactions) and 1033 open banking were both written to cover wallets like PayPal and Venmo. Both rules were later weakened, which reduces near-term compliance cost.
  - The fraud-liability trend still points one way: UK mandatory APP reimbursement, the Zelle suit, the Cash App order, and TD's unmonitored P2P flows. In the UK, payment firms that send Faster Payments are already liable.
- **Wider industry.**
  - Pooled FBO accounts need ledgers that can be reconciled at all times.
  - Third-party concentration (CrowdStrike, Finastra, LockBit at Evolve) is now a supervisory priority under DORA and the OCC.
  - Network economics are contested: Capital One–Discover, DOJ v. Visa, Apple opening NFC, and Pix/UPI-style real-time rails.

## 6. Unresolved problems as of Nov 2026 (evidence-backed)

1. **Synapse victims were not made whole.** More than $65m remained unlocated and no regulator stepped in ([CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/22/synapse-bankruptcy-thousands-of-americans-see-their-savings-vanish.html)). Whether the FDIC custodial rule was ever finalised is not verified here.

## 7. Opportunity signals (all SPECULATIVE)

1. **Ledger-as-a-service for FBO/custodial accounts.** Real-time, bank-reconcilable sub-ledgers with an audit trail per beneficial owner. Evidence: the Synapse shortfall, Evolve's bulk transfers without owner data, and the FDIC's proposed recordkeeping rule.
2. **Compliance tooling for bank-fintech partnerships.** Partner risk scoring, exit plans covering 3rd/4th/5th parties, and AML monitoring sold to sponsor banks. Evidence: the Thread, Sutton, Piermont and Evolve orders.
3. **APP-scam liability infrastructure.** Shared fraud data across sending and receiving firms, behavioural risk scores at the moment of payment, and claim adjudication, sized for a cap like £85k. Evidence: PSR PS24/7, IC3 and UNODC data.
4. **Deepfake-resistant authorisation for treasury and corporate payments.** Out-of-band, cryptographic approval of payment instructions. Evidence: the Arup $25m case and FinCEN's 2024 alert.
5. **Nigeria-specific opportunities.**
   - KYC and address-verification APIs, and BVN/NIN remediation for wallets (the March 2024 freeze, and the June 2024 conditions requiring address and face checks).
   - Agent-network management tools for PTSA routing, cash-out limits and float accounts (Dec 2024 circular).
   - Services for banks raising new capital, such as regulatory reporting, ahead of the March 2026 recapitalisation deadline.
6. **Instant-payment add-ons.** Verification of Payee, request-to-pay, and A2A checkout on Pix, UPI and EU instant rails. Evidence: the EU IPR, Pix's 47% share, and Apple's NFC opening.
7. **Third-party concentration monitoring and failover for small banks and credit unions.** Evidence: CrowdStrike, Patelco, Finastra, and DORA's oversight of critical providers.
