## 1. Key findings (top 10)

1. **The macro regime turned in 2026.** A war in the Middle East disrupted the Strait of Hormuz and pushed up energy prices. The Fed under its new chair, Kevin Warsh (confirmed 13 May), then made its first rate hike since 2023 on 16 Sep, to 3.75–4.00%. It held on 28 Oct with two dissents in favour of another hike. The Fed projects that 2% inflation will not return before 2029. VERIFIED: [Fed statement][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20261028a1.pdf), [CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html), [ECB FSR May 2026][F](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/html/ecb.fsr202605~50566915a7.en.html).
2. **US banks show slow erosion, not a crisis.** Six small banks failed through 25 Sep, the largest being Nano Banc ($736M in assets; $114M estimated cost to the Deposit Insurance Fund). That count is above the two failures in each of 2024 and 2025. In Q2, banks earned $90.1bn and the problem-bank list fell to 47, but unrealized securities losses were still $326.7bn and uninsured deposits grew while insured deposits fell. VERIFIED: [FDIC failed bank list][F](https://www.fdic.gov/bank-failures/failed-bank-list), [FDIC QBP Q2][F](https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2026/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-second-quarter-2026).
3. **US stablecoin law is only half built.** The OCC published the first final GENIUS Act rule on 21 Oct (effective 18 Jan 2027), and the Fed proposed its own rules on 24 Sep. The CLARITY market-structure bill, however, failed cloture 49–50 in September over stablecoin yield and ethics provisions. Democrats then won Congress on 3 Nov. VERIFIED: [WilmerHale][F](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20261027-occ-finalizes-genius-act-rule-for-permitted-payment-stablecoin-issuers-easing-several-key-proposed-requirements-and-raising-new-issues), [American Banker][F](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/crypto-market-structure-bill-fails-in-senate-vote-49-50), [Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/midterm-results-may-curb-bank-friendly-legislation-analysts-say/873759/).
4. **Fintechs are rushing into bank charters.** Approved, conditionally approved or applying in 2026: Nubank, Coinbase, Mercury, Circle (full approval), Revolut, Ripple (final), Block, Ramp, Sezzle and others. The OCC has granted 21 trust charters, 13 of them to crypto firms, and ICBA has sued. Chime chose to buy its partner bank (Stride, for $590M). Wise and Bunq were rejected. VERIFIED: [American Banker][F](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/fintechs-asking-for-and-receiving-bank-charters-in-2026), [Banking Dive (ICBA)][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/icba-sues-occ-gould-over-trust-charters-crypto-banking/832146/).
5. **The CFPB is close to dormant but legally alive.** It started only one public enforcement action between Jul 2025 and Jun 2026. It plans to cut staff to 556, and it has stopped publishing complaint narratives. Three district courts have rejected the administration's "combined earnings" theory for cutting off its funding. VERIFIED: [GovExec][F](https://www.govexec.com/workforce/2026/04/consumer-watchdog-agency-asks-court-permission-slash-its-workforce-two-thirds/412598/), [CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/the-cfpb-to-cease-discretionary-publication-of-complaint-narratives-and-visualizations/), [Ballard Spahr][S](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/09/30/third-district-court-rejects-olcs-interpretation-of-cfpb-funding-statute/).
6. **Agentic commerce is behind its own hype.** OpenAI scaled back ChatGPT Instant Checkout on 24 Mar. Liability rules are still unwritten: EMVCo's "Intent Services" exists only as a draft framework (Sep), and Amex alone offers agent-error protection (Apr). PayPal's CEO expects no meaningful agentic volume before 2028. VERIFIED/REPORTED: [MacRumors][F](https://macrumors.com/2026/03/25/chatgpt-revamps-shopping-features), [EMVCo][F](https://www.emvco.com/knowledge-hub/how-emvco-is-working-to-enable-secure-interoperable-and-scalable-card-based-agentic-payments/), [Payments Dive][F](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/paypal-isnt-agile-enough-ceo-says/833449/).
7. **PayPal is in a turnaround.** The board replaced Alex Chriss with Enrique Lores (announced 3 Feb, effective 1 Mar) and reorganised the company into three units (29 Apr). Stripe and Advent bid $53bn in July and dropped the bid in August. Q3 revenue grew 2% and active accounts were flat at 440M. Through Paga (Jan), PayPal re-entered Nigeria for inbound payments. VERIFIED/REPORTED: [PayPal][F](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2026-02-03-PayPal-Appoints-Enrique-Lores-as-Chief-Executive-Officer-and-David-W-Dorman-as-Independent-Board-Chair), [SEC 8-K][F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000086/pypl3q-26earningsrelease.htm), [Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/how-paypal-may-benefit-stripe-advent-53-billion-offer-venmo/825316/), [TechCabal][F](https://techcabal.com/2026/01/27/paypal-returns-to-nigeria-after-two-decades-this-time-through-paga/).
8. **Fraud is still growing and is increasingly AI-assisted.** FBI IC3 recorded $20.9bn of losses in 2025 (+26%), including $7.2bn from crypto investment fraud and $893M in AI-related losses. UK APP fraud losses rose 19% to £576m, although reimbursement is working (97% of in-scope claims repaid). The CFPB received 6.6M complaints and blames LLM and AI-agent "spurious submissions". VERIFIED: [IC3 2025][F](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf), [UK Finance][F](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/news-and-insight/press-release/fraud-report-2026-press-release), [PSR][F](https://www.psr.org.uk/news-and-updates/latest-news/news/payment-fraud-falls-by-73m-following-psr-reimbursement-scheme/), [CFPB CR report][F](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_2025-cr-annual-report_2026-03.pdf).
9. **Nigeria finished its bank recapitalisation and its fintechs are becoming banks.** N4.65trn was raised, and the CBN reported on 1 Apr that 33 banks met the new requirement (in February only 20 of 33 had). JPMorgan received approval-in-principle for a merchant bank. Paystack and Flutterwave bought microfinance licences, and Kuda and Moniepoint restructured. REPORTED/VERIFIED: [Nairametrics][F](https://nairametrics.com/2026/11/02/sanwo-olu-challenges-nigerian-banks-to-turn-n4-65-trillion-recapitalisation-into-jobs/), [CBN MPC 164][F](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2026/CCD/MPC_Comminique%20No%20164%20Sept%202026%20MPR%20Corridor%20Recalibration.pdf), [TechCabal][F](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/28/flutterwave-ceo-on-fintechs-and-banking/).
10. **Card-fee economics are being reopened on both sides of the Atlantic.** The 8th Circuit vacated the Fed's 21¢ debit-interchange cap (26 Oct). A Visa/Mastercard settlement would drop "honor all cards" (still awaiting approval). The UK PSR has imposed fee-transparency directions on the schemes, and the EU is legislating a digital euro (Parliament mandate 416–169 on 9 Jul) while Wero replaces iDEAL. VERIFIED: [Payments Dive][F](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/fed-loses-debit-card-cap-appeal/872785/), [PSR][F](https://www.psr.org.uk/news-and-updates/latest-news/news/psr-strengthens-oversight-of-mastercard-and-visa-as-card-fees-review-moves-to-implementation/), [Brussels Signal][F](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/07/parliament-clears-digital-euro-talks-after-right-wing-groups-force-a-vote/), [EPI/Wero][F](https://wero-wallet.eu/news/wero-ecommerce-netherlands).

## 3. Detailed findings by sub-topic

### 3.1 Macro and rates: the 2026 regime change
- **What happened.** Most sources describe an orderly Fed hold through H1 2026; the Fed "left rates unchanged at its first five meetings" [S]. Then came a 25bp hike on 16 Sep 2026 to 3.75–4.00%, the first since July 2023, by a 12–0 vote. The September projections put 2026 headline PCE at 3.7% and core at 3.4%, with 2% inflation not reached until 2029. The 30-year mortgage rate had reached 7.19% ([CNBC][F](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html)). On 28 Oct the FOMC held 10–2; Cook and Hammack wanted another hike. The statement says "Inflation remains elevated", and IORB is 3.90% ([Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20261028a1.pdf)). VERIFIED.
- **Why.** The ECB's May FSR describes "the unexpected outbreak of war in the Middle East" with "disruptions to shipping through the Strait of Hormuz and attacks on energy infrastructure". It also notes earlier 2026 shocks: questions over Greenland's sovereignty, a US military intervention in Venezuela, concerns about central bank independence, and the Supreme Court's decision overturning US "reciprocal" tariffs ([ECB FSR][F](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/html/ecb.fsr202605~50566915a7.en.html)). CNBC attributes the hike to Iran-war fuel costs plus lingering tariff effects. VERIFIED.
- **Fed leadership and independence.** Warsh was confirmed 54–45 on 13 May, the most partisan vote for a Fed chair. Powell remains a governor. A DOJ probe of the Fed was dropped in April ([Roll Call][S](https://rollcall.com/2026/05/13/warsh-confirmed-to-lead-federal-reserve/)). REPORTED.
- **Consequences.** Higher-for-longer rates keep a floor under deposit costs and support net interest margins (Q2 NIM 3.32%). They also extend unrealized securities losses ($326.7bn) and squeeze leveraged consumers and CRE borrowers. The hike reverses the easing many fintech lenders had budgeted for. INTERPRETATION.

### 3.2 US bank health, failures, credit quality
- **Failures (six through 25 Sep).** Metropolitan Capital, Chicago (30 Jan; $19.7M DIF cost); CB&T–West Georgia (1 May; ~$97M); Kentland FS&LA (10 Jul; ~$1.2M); Small Business Bank, Lenexa (17 Jul; $73M assets); Tioga-Franklin, Philadelphia (21 Aug; $5.5M est.); Nano Banc, Irvine (25 Sep; $736M assets; ~$114M DIF cost; Sunwest assumed deposits) ([FDIC list][F](https://www.fdic.gov/bank-failures/failed-bank-list); [American Banker][F](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/fifth-bank-fails-in-2026-as-drip-of-small-bank-closures-continues); [American Banker/DFPI][S](https://dfpi.ca.gov/press_release/california-seizes-nano-banc/)). Causes were idiosyncratic. California cited "a multi-year pattern of executive mismanagement" at Nano Banc, which had a history of CRE-concentration enforcement. The FDIC OIG found that Tioga-Franklin failed after a costly mortgage expansion and a failed search for investors ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/mortgage-business-expansion-killed-tioga-franklin-fdic-oig/873470/)). VERIFIED. No large-bank failures or runs were found in this slice.
- **QBP Q2 2026 (released 25 Aug).** Net income $90.1bn (+12% QoQ). ROA 1.37%. Noncurrent loans $129.7bn. Past-due and nonaccrual rate 1.44%; net charge-offs 0.57%. The PDNA rate on non-owner-occupied CRE at banks over $250bn was 3.08%, down from a 4.99% peak in Q3 2024 but still about 5× pre-pandemic. Problem banks 47. DIF reserve ratio 1.48%. Uninsured deposits rose $317bn while insured deposits fell 1.0%. Earnings were "boosted by one-time equity security gains and trading revenue" ([FDIC][F](https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2026/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-second-quarter-2026)). VERIFIED.
- **Stress tests.** All 32 banks passed on 24 Jun with $708bn of modeled losses, about $200bn of it from cards. Aggregate capital fell 1.6pp under a scenario with CRE −39%, house prices −30% and 10% unemployment ([Fed][F](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20260624a.htm)). On 30 Sep the Fed finalised rules that average the stress capital buffer over two years and put scenarios out for public comment ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/fed-stress-test-changes-transparency-volatility-scenarios-capital-buffer-requirements-bowman-barr/831787/)). VERIFIED. Critics, including Forbes columnists [S], argue the test misses private-credit exposure.
- **Consumer credit (NY Fed Q2, Aug).** Household debt $18.8trn. 4.7% of debt is delinquent (−0.1pp). The student-loan 90+ day rate rose to 10.6%. Median credit score on new auto loans fell 7 points. 137k new bankruptcies ([FRBNY][F](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/interactives/householdcredit/data/pdf/HHDC_2026Q2.pdf)). VERIFIED. The headline is stable, but student loans and subprime auto are the weak spots. INTERPRETATION.
- **Private credit.** The ESAs (23 Sep), the FSB Chair's letter to the G20 (Oct) and the ECB FSR (May) all flag private credit as a vulnerability ([ESAs][F](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/esas-call-vigilance-over-external-dependencies-cyber-threats-and-private-credit-risks), [FSB][F](https://www.fsb.org/2026/10/fsb-chair-sets-out-fsb-work-on-ai-and-stablecoins/)). VERIFIED. The numbers on redemptions and gates are snippet-only. Fitch: non-traded BDC redemption requests rose from 1.6% of NAV in Q3 2025 to 4.5% in Q4 2025 [S](https://www.usbank.com/investing/financial-perspectives/market-news/private-credit.html). Secondary trackers claim Q2 2026 requests reached $15.6bn, a 6.1% default rate, and a Fed inquiry into bank exposure in April 2026 [S, unverified]. CBRE argues the stress is concentrated in software direct lending [S](https://www.cbre.com/insights/briefs/private-credit-stress-contained-or-contagious). REPORTED or DISPUTED as to magnitude.

### 3.3 US regulation and enforcement
- **Capital.** The 19 Mar re-proposals would cut standardized-approach CET1 requirements by about 4.8–6.4% and mortgage RWA by about 30%. The G-SIB surcharge would fall about 3.8%. For Category I/II banks the Basel III endgame piece on its own would add about 1.4%, mainly from trading. Comments closed 18 Jun ([Arnold & Porter][F](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2026/03/agencies-capital-proposals-seek-to-reduce-regulatory-burden)). JPMorgan still estimates it would need about $20bn more and calls the surcharge "still broken" ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/jpmorgan-dimon-capital-proposals-basel-gsib-earnings/817454/)). No final rule had been found as of November. VERIFIED.
- **CFPB.**
  - *Staffing.* The plan is to keep 556 staff, down from more than 1,700 in January 2025. Enforcement would fall 80% and supervision 85% versus Trump's first day. The administration cites OBBBA funding cuts, and an injunction still blocks the RIFs ([GovExec][F](https://www.govexec.com/workforce/2026/04/consumer-watchdog-agency-asks-court-permission-slash-its-workforce-two-thirds/412598/)).
  - *Enforcement.* The CFPB dropped 41–47 cases ([CFA][S](https://consumerfed.org/media/q3khdl10/cfpb-dropped-enforcement-actions-july-2026-update.pdf)), and its semiannual report shows one public action between Jul 2025 and Jun 2026 [S].
  - *Funding.* Three district courts have held that "combined earnings" means Fed revenue, not profit, and the Ninth Circuit appeal is pending [S]. A House GOP bill would move the CFPB onto appropriations (Sep) ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/republicans-unveil-bill-to-change-cfpb-funding-structure/829453/)).
  - *Leadership.* Nominee Brian Johnson cleared committee 13–11. No floor vote had been found as of 4 Nov [S].
  - *Complaints.* The CFPB now routes credit-report disputes to the credit bureaus first, and stopped publishing complaint narratives on 14 Aug ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/the-cfpb-to-cease-discretionary-publication-of-complaint-narratives-and-visualizations/)).
  - VERIFIED for the [F] items above.
- **Open banking (Section 1033).** The 2024 rule is enjoined, and the reconsideration that began with the Aug 2025 ANPR is still unfinished. A revised proposal was reportedly sent to OIRA around 6 Aug 2026 but had not been published as of September [S](https://www.openbankingtracker.com/guides/section-1033-status). JPMorgan now has paid data-access deals with Plaid, Yodlee, Morningstar and Akoya [S]. REPORTED. The question of whether banks may charge for data access remains open.
- **Debanking.** On 14 Oct the OCC said nine banks (JPM, BofA, Citi, Wells, U.S. Bank, Capital One, PNC, TD, BMO) had policies between 2020 and 2025 that "may have required, encouraged or otherwise influenced" debanking. The CFPB launched investigations under EO 14331 ([CFPB][F](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-announces-new-initiative-to-address-occ-uncovered-debanking-by-large-banks/); [Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/occ-debanking-report-trump-gould-jpmorgan-bofa-capital-one-td-cfpb/832997/)). VERIFIED. The finding is politically framed and is likely to be contested. INTERPRETATION.
- **AML enforcement.** AML enforcement continued even as consumer enforcement fell. Amex: OCC cease-and-desist plus $350M (8 Oct) ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/amex-dinged-by-fed-occ-must-pay-350m-for-aml-failures/832570/)). Block: a $964M pact including $764M to resolve DOJ charges that its AML program was ineffective (4 Nov) ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/block-pay-964-million-doj/873765/)). VERIFIED.
- **Payments rules.** On 26 Oct the 8th Circuit held that Regulation II improperly counted fixed costs and vacated the 21¢ debit cap ([Payments Dive][F](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/fed-loses-debit-card-cap-appeal/872785/)). The Visa/Mastercard merchant settlement would end "honor all cards" and widen surcharging, pending court approval ([Payments Dive][F](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/will-ditching-honor-all-cards-rule-matter-visa-mastercard-amex/873187/)). On 19 May an executive order ordered a review of fintech access to Fed rails ([Banking Dive][F](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/trump-order-seeks-open-fed-rails-fintech-waller-fta/820740/)). ## 5. Evolution vs. earlier years (from this slice's vantage)
- **2023 to 2026, failures and regulation.** The 2023 large-bank runs gave way to a 2026 drip of micro-failures with idiosyncratic causes. Supervision moved from tightening (the 2023 Basel proposal sought about +19% capital) to easing (the 2026 re-proposal reduces requirements). Stress-test buffers are now averaged.
- **CFPB.** The CFPB went from its most active enforcement period (2023–24) to near-zero output in 2026, while states (22 AGs) and the courts became the main counterweight. The 3 Nov midterm results may reverse the political direction from 2027.
- **Stablecoins.** Stablecoins moved from enforcement-led treatment (2023) to statute (GENIUS, Jul 2025) to implementing rules (2026). Market-structure law remains stuck.
- **Agentic commerce.** It went from 2025 announcements (OpenAI–PayPal, Perplexity, Mastercard Agent Pay) to a 2026 reality check.
- **Fraud.** IC3 losses grew from $12.5bn (2023) to $16.6bn (2024) to $20.9bn (2025) [F, IC3 chart]. UK APP fraud fell after mandatory reimbursement began in late 2024 and rose again in 2025, driven by investment scams.
- **Nigeria.** Nigeria moved from the 2023 naira crisis and 2024 recapitalisation announcement to a 2026 completion, with inflation falling to about 15% and reserves at $55bn.

## 6. Implications for PayPal and the industry
- **PayPal.**
  - *Core business.* Branded checkout is losing share to Stripe and Apple Pay (William Blair: Stripe processes about 40% more volume [F]) and to agentic interfaces it does not control. The pullback of the OpenAI checkout it powered weakens that bet. The turnaround depends on Venmo and consumer financial services monetisation, and on cutting about 20% of staff (REPORTED).
  - *Stablecoins.* The OCC rule clarifies the PYUSD issuer framework (Paxos is the issuer). Yield on stablecoin balances ("rewards" are offered on PYUSD) is the politically contested feature after CLARITY failed and Democrats won Congress. INTERPRETATION.
  - *Fees and liability.* The vacated debit cap and the end of "honor all cards" could change merchant economics and wallet routing. PSD3's treatment of APP fraud as unauthorised, and UK reimbursement rules, raise wallet fraud liability in Europe.
  - *Africa.* The Paga model, inbound-only with local settlement through a licensed partner, is a template for re-entering high-fraud markets.
- **Industry.**
  - The bank charter is now the strategic asset: fintechs are buying or applying for banks, and banks are building their own coins.
  - AI is cutting back-office and compliance jobs (StanChart, Amex, Fiserv, Chime) while creating new fraud, complaint-flooding and cyber risks (IC3 AI losses, CFPB "AI agents", Mythos).
  - Geopolitics (Iran war, energy shock) is now a direct source of credit, cyber and rate risk.

## 7. Unresolved problems as of Nov 2026 (evidence-backed)
1. **No liability framework for AI-agent purchases.** EMVCo has only a draft framework, Amex has the only protection, and EWS says there are no "rules of the road" [F].
2. **APP and investment-scam losses keep rising.** UK APP +19%, US crypto-investment fraud $7.2bn, 60+ victims $7.7bn. Tech and telecom platforms, where 66% of UK APP cases originate, bear no reimbursement duty [F].
3. **The US consumer complaint system is degraded.** 6.6M mostly credit-reporting complaints, many spurious; narratives are no longer published; the CFPB has minimal enforcement [F].
4. **Open banking (1033) is in legal limbo,** with bank data-access fees spreading [S].
5. **Stablecoin yield and market structure are unresolved** after CLARITY failed. FDIC and Treasury GENIUS rules are pending. GENIUS takes effect on 18 Jan 2027 [F].
6. **The CFPB's funding, leadership and staffing are unresolved.** The Ninth Circuit appeal and Johnson's floor vote are pending, and the RIF injunction stands [S/F].
7. **Remittances to Sub-Saharan Africa still cost 8.46%,** the highest of any region [F, secondary].
8. **Private credit is opaque and bank linkages are unmeasured.** ESAs, FSB and ECB warnings exist, but there is no verified exposure data [F/S].
9. **Single points of failure:** M-Pesa in Kenya and core-processor concentration in the US [F].
10. **UK motor finance redress is stuck** pending Upper Tribunal challenges in Feb–Mar 2027 [S/F].
11. **Digital-lending abuse** in Ghana and Nigeria: unlicensed loan apps and spam-call debt collection (TechCabal headline [F list]).
